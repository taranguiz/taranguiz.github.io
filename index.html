I'm a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Colorado Boulder, studying how faults, climate, and erosion shape landscapes over time. I combine field observations, remote sensing, geochronology, and numerical modeling to understand surface processes across timescales — from active tectonics to long-term landscape evolution.

Research

Strike-slip faults, landscape evolution, and geomorphic markers of tectonics and climate.
