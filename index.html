I'm a geomorphologist captivated by how tectonics, climate, and surface processes shape topography. I am currently a Lucas J. Kimes Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Earth Science at the University of Colorado Boulder.

Research themes

Active strike-slip faults & landscape response

Slowly slipping strike-slip faults leave measurable records in topography near the fault and in the upstream drainage network, even under conditions of extreme aridity.

Climate, sediment & landscape evolution

Numerical models help test how climate variability and sediment cover influence river dynamics along synthetic strike-slip faults.

Long-term exhumation & mountain building

Low-temperature thermochronology reveal cooling, exhumation, pull-apart basin development, and landscape evolution over 150 millions of years in the Coastal Range of Northern Chile.

Effect of GIA in post-glacial landscapes

Can we couple GIA and LEM models to better understand how topography respond to the spatially and temporally variable uplift that follows deglaciation?
